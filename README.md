# Monster of the Week — Campaign Keeper

A free, browser-based campaign wiki for Keepers. No accounts, no server, no cost.

## Entry templates
Mysteries · Monsters · Minions · Phenomena · Villains · Locations · Bystanders · Session Log

- Picking a standard **type** (e.g. Monster → *Tempter*) fills in its motivation. You can type your own.
- **Harm boxes** and **countdown steps** are clickable on an entry page for live tracking.
- Link entries with the **link pickers** or by writing `[[Entry Name]]` in any text box.
- Every entry shows what **references it**.
- **Tags** (`#woods`) and full-text **search** from the sidebar.
- Multiple campaigns.

## Where data lives
In your browser (localStorage). Use **Export** to save a `.json` backup and **Import** to load it on another device or share with a co-Keeper.

## Free hosting (GitHub Pages)
1. Repo **Settings → Pages**.
2. Source: **Deploy from a branch** → branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
3. Open `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

To run locally, just open `index.html` in a browser.
